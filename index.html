Fat Melt

A personal calorie and weight tracker that predicts how much fat you'll melt this week.

What it does

  • Logs daily food and calories, and turns each finished day's calorie deficit into an estimate of fat melted.
  • Shows a weekly calorie budget and a forecast for the week and the next 30 days.
  • Charts weight, fat mass and body fat % over time.

How it uses Google

Fat Melt can sign in with Google to read your weight and body fat measurements from Google Health. Access is read-only: Fat Melt never writes to, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Fat Melt runs only on its owner's own computer. Your Google data goes straight to that computer and stays there. It is not uploaded to any server, and it is not shared with anyone.
