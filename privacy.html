Privacy Policy

Fat Melt · Last updated October 18, 2026

Fat Melt is a personal calorie and weight tracker. It runs as a small program on its owner's own computer and is only reachable from that computer. This policy explains what data Fat Melt uses and what happens to it.

Data Fat Melt reads from Google

When you choose to connect Google, Fat Melt asks for read-only access to your Google Health measurements (the health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly scope). It uses that access only to read:

  • your weight measurements, and
  • your body fat percentage measurements.

Fat Melt does not read any other Google data, and it never writes to, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

How the data is used

Weight and body fat readings are used only to show your charts, trends and fat-loss forecasts inside Fat Melt.

Where the data is stored

  • Measurements and food logs are stored only on the computer running Fat Melt, in that computer's web browser storage.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored in a private file on that same computer, so Fat Melt can sync without asking you to sign in every time.
  • Nothing is sent to any server run by Fat Melt. There is no Fat Melt server, account system or cloud database.

Sharing

Fat Melt does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Fat Melt's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Use Disconnect in Fat Melt to delete the stored Google sign-in token.
  • You can also remove Fat Melt's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clearing the browser's site data for Fat Melt deletes all locally stored measurements and food logs.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised at github.com/devin260-dev/fat-melt/issues.
